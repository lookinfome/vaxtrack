About VaxTrack

VaxTrack is a two-dose vaccination management platform. It gives people one place to book and track their vaccination journey, gives hospitals self-service control over their own slot capacity, and gives program administrators full oversight — with a record of who did what, and why, on every action. Nothing here replaces medical advice; it's simply here to make the logistics of getting vaccinated a little less stressful, for everyone involved.

Coming Soon

A Help Center is on its way. v1.3.0 shipped a Support module for raising tickets, but left it unfinished — no attachments, no status tracker. v2.0.0 is bringing it back from the ground up, powered by the same live notifications and audit trail already running through the rest of the app, so you'll always know where your request stands.
